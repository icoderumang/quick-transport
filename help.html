Frequently Asked Questions

Booking a ride is simple! Click on "Book Ride" in the navigation bar. Enter your pickup and drop-off locations, choose your vehicle type (Bike, Auto, Car, or SUV), and click "Confirm Booking". A driver will be assigned to you immediately.

Yes! On the booking page, you can optionally select a future date and time for your ride. Your ride will be confirmed and a driver assigned closer to the scheduled time.

Go to Ride History, find the ride you want to cancel, and click the "Cancel" button. Note that cancellations made after a driver has been assigned may incur a small cancellation fee of ₹30.

We offer four vehicle types: Bike (₹7/km, 1 passenger), Auto (₹10/km, 3 passengers), Car (₹15/km, 4 passengers), and SUV (₹22/km, 6 passengers). All fares include a base fare of ₹30.

We accept Cash, UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, and our QT Wallet. You can set your preferred payment method in the Payments section. For online payments, we support all major banks and UPI apps like GPay, PhonePe, and Paytm.

The QT Wallet is a digital wallet integrated into your account. You can add money to it via UPI, Card, or Net Banking, and use it for instant payments. Wallet payments are instant, secure, and you get exclusive cashback offers when paying via wallet.

For cancelled rides paid online, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. Wallet payments are refunded instantly. If you have a billing dispute, contact our support team below and we'll resolve it within 24 hours.

Yes! Go to the Payments page and enter your promo code in the "Promo Code" section. Active codes: FIRST50 (50% off first ride), RIDE20 (₹20 off any ride), QT100 (₹100 wallet cashback on adding ₹500+).

Safety is our top priority. All drivers undergo background verification, document checks, and vehicle inspections. You can share your live trip with trusted contacts and access our SOS emergency button during the ride.

Press the SOS button in the app immediately. This alerts our emergency team and can notify local authorities. You can also call our 24/7 emergency helpline at 1800-QT-SAFE (1800-87-7233).

Go to your Profile page, click "Security Settings", and select "Change Password". You'll need to enter your current password and then your new one. For forgotten passwords, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.

Visit your Profile page and click on "Edit Profile". You can update your phone number after OTP verification on your registered number. Note that phone number changes may require re-verification for security.
